# AccessReady 3D — what the Lam lens changed

Source plan: the attached development plan (also `/workspace/AccessReady-3D-original.docx`). Amended file: `docs/AccessReady-3D-Project-Development-Plan.docx`.

The idea is unchanged: a browser route for one Hong Kong campus building, staff-owned access status, malls only later. Voice stays first person. Mapxus remains a hypothesis, not a claimed win.

## Added, from the lecture frameworks

- Pain and payer before the renderer. Visitors use the route free. The hypothesized payer is a facilities budget owner (LN07: stage before features).
- Desirability, feasibility, and viability each have a kill: no recognised pain, no named editor or unchecked routes, no purchasing step, and then no mall (LN04).
- Whole product is written first (floor, entrance, lift, honest “no verified route”, bilingual 2D and text, staff owner and expiry). The MVP is a cut of that list. Positioning, accounts, and evacuation stay out (LN06).
- Jobs, pains, and gains for visitor, facilities staff, and budget owner are prose, not a grid.
- Stage clock: this plan is before the first dollar. Breakeven on cash is separated from breakeven on founder hours. The ROI point (payback of about HK$5,000 cash plus about HK$20,000 of time) is stated as beyond the course (LN07, LN10).
- Short TAM / SAM / SOM with every count labeled as an assumption: eight UGC-funded universities, three buildings, then two sites, then one paid building or zero. Ceiling arithmetic HK$648,000 is not called a measured market (LN02).
- “Done” is checked routes plus a five-minute seeded closure by a named editor. A demo is not that (LN12).
- The ask is one evaluation review and one introduction to a facilities budget owner. It explicitly does not ask the lecturer to fund, buy, or certify.

## Trimmed
