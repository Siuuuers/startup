## Investment behavior that is evidenced

His stated operating list (31 Dec 2023): source the best potential impact projects; full support to investees; training and advisory; gain return on the existing plan.

Sectors he has actually stood next to, as Gravity's presenter in March 2023, not as a published personal thesis: medical wearable (sleep apnea), food hardware (hot-chain bento), indoor agriculture, co-living, shared mobility with data, smart lockers. That set is health, food, housing, mobility, logistics. It rhymes with LSCM (logistics), his biomedical courses, and the LN12 classroom cases (marine sensors, plant-based food). It is not a mandate document.

Sengital, the company he founded, is hardware and systems: MEMS motion, wireless, certificates, embedded and phone apps, later described on LinkedIn as AI and IoT for business processes. His courses at CUHK MAE are robotics commercialization; CityU BME is clinical AI; PolyU EEE is AIoT. The lecture examples he wrote numbers for are IoT sensor modules, a heart-rate wearable, and a robot BOM with ROS and an edge computer.

Inferred, and labeled as inference: the fields he keeps rebuilding are sensing, connected hardware, robotics, biomedical signals, logistics, and impact categories a Hong Kong grant or a university lab can touch. He has not published "I do not invest in X."

Cheque size: not found for his personal angel deals.
