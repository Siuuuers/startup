## Slide 14

Practical Examples of Market and Competitive Analysis
                                                                             Consider a startup developing IoT sensor modules targeting industrial
                                                                             automation in Hong Kong. The first step is market sizing using
                                                                             TAM/SAM/SOM calculations. Assume total IoT device market TAM is
                                                                             approximately HKD 50 billion regionally.
                                                                             Targeting industrial automation yielding 30% of TAM results in SAM of:
                                                                                            𝑆𝐴𝑀 = 50 billion × 0.3 = 15 billion HKD
                                                                             Estimating realistic market share at 5% due to competitive intensity
                                                                             gives SOM:
                                                                                           𝑆𝑂𝑀 = 15 billion × 0.05 = 0.75 billion HKD
                                                                             Next, analyzing competitors reveals dominant firms with 40% market
                                                                             share controlling supply chain access, indicating the startup’s need to
                                                                             innovate beyond price competition.
                                                                             Applying Porter’s Five Forces quantitatively:
                                                                             •   Rivalry intensity: High, with over 10 competitors.
                                                                             •   Threat of new entrants: Moderate, due to high capital
                                                                                 requirements.
                                                                             •   Supplier power: High for unique components.
                                                                             •   Buyer power: Moderate; industrial customers demand
                                                                                 customization.
                                                                             •   Substitutes: Emerging wireless sensor solutions.
                                                                             Employing these analyses guides strategic decisions such as product
                                                                             differentiation and alliances with suppliers to mitigate risks and
                                                                             capitalize on market opportunities.

## Slide 38

Mathematical Formulas for TAM/SAM/SOM
                                                                             The market size metrics TAM, SAM, and SOM can be expressed
                                                                             mathematically. Understanding these formulas supports accurate
                                                                             and transparent calculations.
                                                                             Let:
                                                                               • 𝑁𝑡𝑜𝑡𝑎𝑙 = Total possible customers in the relevant market
                                                                               • 𝑃 = Price per unit or average revenue per customer annually
                                                                               • 𝐹𝑇𝐴𝑀 = Fraction of total customers in the overall market
                                                                                 (usually 1 for TAM)
                                                                               • 𝐹𝑆𝐴𝑀 = Fraction of TAM served by company's product/service
                                                                                 capabilities and reach
                                                                               • 𝐹𝑆𝑂𝑀 = Fraction of SAM realistically obtainable
                                                                             Then,
                                                                                                 TAM = 𝑁𝑡𝑜𝑡𝑎𝑙 × 𝑃 × 𝐹𝑇𝐴𝑀
                                                                                                   SAM = TAM × 𝐹𝑆𝐴𝑀
                                                                                                   SOM = SAM × 𝐹𝑆𝑂𝑀
                                                                             Alternatively, if starting from unit volume:
                                                                                   Market Size = Number of Units Sold × Price per Unit
                                                                             In the bottom-up model, the number of units can be estimated by:
                                                                              Number of Units Sold
                                                                              = Number of Customers × Average Units Bought per Customer
                                                                             Each fraction 𝐹𝑆𝐴𝑀 and 𝐹𝑆𝑂𝑀 must be supported by market
                                                                             research data or validated assumptions about reach and
                                                                             competitive share.

## Slide 39

Step-by-step Numerical Examples of Market Size Calculations
                To clarify these concepts, consider a startup developing a wearable heart rate
                monitor targeting the Hong Kong market, an example aligned with technology
                applications in Electrical and Electronic Engineering.
                Step 1: Calculate TAM
                                              • Total population of Hong Kong: approximately 7.5 million.
                                              • Target customers: Adults aged 18-65 = approx. 5 million.
                                              • Estimated percentage interested in health monitoring devices = 60%.
                                              • Potential customers = 5,000,000 x 0.60 = 3,000,000.
                                              • Average price per device = HKD 2,000.
                Therefore,
                                                            TAM = 3,000,000 × 2,000 = 𝐻𝐾𝐷6,000,000,000
                Step 2: Estimate SAM
                                              • The startup plans to initially serve urban areas with tech-savvy population
                                                which accounts for 50% of the TAM segment.
                                              • Hence,
                                                 SAM = TAM × 0.50 = 𝐻𝐾𝐷6,000,000,000 × 0.50 = 𝐻𝐾𝐷3,000,000,000
                Step 3: Estimate SOM
                                              • Realistically expecting to capture 2% of SAM in the next 3 years based on
                                                marketing and sales capabilities.
                                              • Thus,
                                                  SOM = SAM × 0.02 = 𝐻𝐾𝐷3,000,000,000 × 0.02 = 𝐻𝐾𝐷60,000,000
