## Slide 42

Lecture 12) Startup Pitch Deck
                                                                      Case Study 1: NerOcean
                                                                         NerOcean is a sensor company to provide cost-effective
                                                                         solution for sustainable environmental monitoring and
                                                                         management. The uniqueness of NerOcean's sensors are to
                                                                         reduce the sensors cost by 80% and maintenance cost by
                                                                         90%. With our sensors, we can convert the point
                                                                         measurement limited by the current methods into 3-
                                                                         dimensional high-resolution monitoring of a large water body
                                                                         with less maintenance cost, especially in remote areas. We
                                                                         are collaborating with AFCD and the State Key Laboratory of
                                                                         Marine Pollution (SKLMP). We will provide our sensors to
                                                                         AFCD for aquaculture zone water oxygen monitoring and
                                                                         SKLMP for their marine research at >100 major estuarines
                                                                         over the world under their UN-endorsed 10-year project.

Lecture 12) Startup Pitch Deck
                                                                 Case Study 2: Meat the Next
                                                                         Founded in 2020, Meat the Next (MTN) specializes in plant-
                                                                         based food technology and aim to solve food shortages by
                                                                         creating New Generation Food. New Generation Food is not
                                                                         only a better way to feed the population, it also brings
                                                                         positive impact to the planet, the environment and the
                                                                         climate.

                                                                         By introducing novel technologies, MTN plans to further
                                                                         explore the potential of sustainable environmental
                                                                         technologies and to act as an industry leader in reducing
                                                                         carbon emission. They will also proactively investigate the
                                                                         commercial potential of carbon deduction. More than just
                                                                         food, the technology makes sustainable lifestyle possible.
