## Slide 53

Value proposition canvas
                                       • The value proposition canvas mainly tests the preliminary desirability of technology
                                         (product, service);
                                       • The information on the right side of the value proposition canvas (RHS) often comes
                                         from market research, which needs to be objective and truthful, and often cannot be
                                         adapted at will. The content of the left semicircle (LHS) should be changed to match
                                         the right (RHS) mainly through technical (product, service) positioning, selection,
                                         improvement, etc.
                                       • The value proposition is relative to the existing solution being used; If two customers
                                         use two different existing solutions (resulting in different RHS, different matching
                                         situations), two different VPCs need to be mapped. As a result, the value proposition
                                         of the final choice tends to vary.
                                       • One technology (service, company) may not solve all the pain and benefits, so it may
                                         be necessary to determine which pains/benefits to prioritize.
                                       • Trade-offs: While increasing some benefits, some new pain points may be introduced;
                                         Or when alleviating some pain points, it may also reduce some benefits.
