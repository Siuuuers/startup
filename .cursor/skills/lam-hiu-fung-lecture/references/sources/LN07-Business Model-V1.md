Business Cycle

After the business growing stage, the business is a proven business and enter the
business cycle. Business cycle is a cycle of expansion and contraction that business
undergo over time. In general, the business cycle consists of 6 distinct phases: expansion;
peak; recession; depression, trough and recovery.

Business Cycle

When business from growing stage (phase 0) to business expansion (phase 1) reaches the
peak point (phase 2), it will enter the recession (phase 3) and then depression (phase 4). It
means that the business will enter downside. Then it will reach the trough point (phase 5).
Then it will start the recovery (phase 6) to enter the expansion (phase 1) again.
