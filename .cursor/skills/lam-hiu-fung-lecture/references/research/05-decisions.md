## What the classroom model does

Separate the bio from the teaching plan.

- LN07 defines three times: first order, monthly breakeven, cumulative net profit equal to the amount invested. He asks which stage an investor would buy. He does not mark an answer.
- LN06: whole product for one buying reason, then cut the MVP from that list.
- LN02: TAM/SAM/SOM in HKD with every fraction labeled. Worked examples are IoT sensor modules and a Hong Kong heart-rate wearable.
- LN04: desirability, feasibility, viability as tests of business-model assumptions, including for researchers leaving the lab.
- LN10: a robotic startup's monthly plan. University funding (TSSSU labeled in the margin), later government funding, service and component first-dollar marks, SAFE, a Series A line, dividends, ROI point on the dividend line in year 3. Teaching plan, not a term sheet he signed.
- LN12: three deck frames, one of them "Alan's Scenario-based Framework"; One Second Theorem; Zero Mistake Theorem; demo of one core workflow. Cases on the slides include NerOcean and Meat the Next.
