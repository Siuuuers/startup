## He asks the room, and leaves the answer open

First-hand, LN07:

- "What is the stage of your startup? Startup? Scale up? Mature?"
- "If you were an investor, which stage do you want to invest? Why?"
- "Which stage is the lowest cost? Which stage is the lowest risk? Which stage has the significant increase in cost?"
- On the business cycle: which phase would you invest in, and "Is fundraising suitable for sustainable development?"

He turns the student into the investor before they ask for money. The slides do not mark a correct option. LN01, LN10, and LN12 end with the same prompt: summarize, then apply it to your project as an artifact.
